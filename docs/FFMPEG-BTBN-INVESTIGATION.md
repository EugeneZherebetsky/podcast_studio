# BtbN FFmpeg candidate — research only, not cleared to redistribute

The separately downloaded [Windows x64 LGPL shared FFmpeg 8.1-family archive](https://github.com/FFmpeg-Builds/FFmpeg-Builds/releases/download/latest/ffmpeg-n8.1-latest-win64-lgpl-shared-8.1.zip) was examined on 2026-11-14. GitHub [release API](https://api.github.com/repos/FFmpeg-Builds/FFmpeg-Builds/releases/tags/latest) reported asset ID 743681424 and SHA-256 `04a6e33900868e7e5b1742cd76a0e92ecbb146e053ad61fef599aa25e4de3c62`; the downloaded 80,303,976-byte archive independently matched. **The release reports `immutable: false` and `latest` moves; neither the URL nor availability is immutable.** A hash identifies bytes but does not ensure future availability. The build was not included in this project or installer.

The archive contains ffmpeg, ffprobe and ffplay executables; FFmpeg DLLs `avdevice-62.dll`, `avcodec-62.dll`, `swresample-6.dll`, `swscale-9.dll`, `avformat-62.dll`, `avfilter-11.dll` and `avutil-60.dll`; headers, import libraries, documentation, presets and `LICENSE.txt`. The executables report `n8.1.3-35-gf1acb0a266-20261113`, GCC 16.2.0 and crosstool-NG 1.29.0.36_fbb6372. The reported configure options include `--enable-version3 --enable-shared --disable-static --pkg-config-flags=--static` and many third-party libraries. Shared FFmpeg DLLs do not prove every dependency is dynamically linked or establish the licenses and exact sources of their contents. The `loudnorm` filter and `ipod` muxer were listed; a one-second generated sine wave encoded with `loudnorm`, AAC and the ipod muxer successfully and ffprobe reported duration 1.000000. This is not an end-to-end podcast generation test. The packaged Electron runtime separately contains its own `ffmpeg.dll`; that is not the candidate command-line build and is also subject to the separate Electron licensing audit.

Neither exact source archives, corresponding complete dependency versions and patches, license/copyright notices for every component, nor durable recipient-accessible source delivery and applicable relinking arrangements were established. The archive's single license file is not evidence that its numerous configured external libraries have all required notices. The [build project](https://github.com/FFmpeg-Builds/FFmpeg-Builds) scripts and the [maintainer discussion about source caching](https://github.com/FFmpeg-Builds/FFmpeg-Builds/issues/678) do not by themselves establish those items for these bytes. [FFmpeg legal guidance](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html) calls for corresponding source and configuration, among other requirements. No conclusion about legal compliance is made; do not redistribute this candidate until provenance, all applicable notices, source/relinking and independent legal review are complete. Existing separate Node/Electron notices and D5 and data-copy gates also remain open.
